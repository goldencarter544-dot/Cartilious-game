# The Food Outbreak Plan

## Goal

Build a simple first version of **The Food Outbreak** without making the project too hard too early.

## Game Plan

1. Keep the current movement and camera so the game stays easier to build.
2. Turn the starter game into a small reskin first:
   - rename the game
   - change the level goal to "beat enemy, then enter door"
   - keep coins
3. Build **Level 1** as the test level:
   - one food enemy
   - three coins
   - one door
   - simple platforms
4. Change the visuals in this order:
   - level art and background
   - player art for Quayshawn Banks
   - food enemy art
   - door art
5. Add the first gameplay rules:
   - player defeats enemies by jumping on them
   - door stays locked until enemies are beaten
   - falling resets the level
   - health system later
6. Add the first UI:
   - game title
   - coin count
   - enemies left
   - simple win message
7. Add placeholder sounds:
   - jump
   - coin pickup
   - enemy defeat
   - level complete
8. Test Level 1 until it feels fun.
9. After Level 1 works, expand to:
   - more food enemies
   - more levels
   - power-ups like baseball bat and football pads
   - bosses and the Food King

## Result

- **Version 1 plan**: one polished level, not all 5 levels at once
- **Main goal**: make it feel like your game fast
- **Best first build**: Quayshawn Banks, food enemy, coins, locked door, dark sports/city theme
- **Save for later**: multiple characters, bosses, voice lines, save system, advanced powers

The smartest move is to win one inning first:

Make **one good level** that works, then grow it.
