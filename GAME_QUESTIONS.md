Answer these questions to help shape the game revamp.

## Main Character

6. Who is the main character?



7. Is the character human, animal, robot, monster, or something else?



8. What does the character look like?



9. What special move or power should the character have?



10. Should the character feel cute, cool, funny, scary, heroic, or goofy?



11. Do you want one playable character or multiple?



12. If there are multiple characters, should they play differently?



## Scene And World

13. Where does the game take place?



14. What should the first level look like?



15. What kind of world do you want: forest, cave, city, sky, castle, ice, volcano, lab, or something original?



16. Should the background feel bright and happy or dark and dangerous?



17. Do you want one world style or several worlds?



18. Are there any special scene effects you want?



## Levels

19. How many levels do you want in the first version?



20. Should levels be short and fast or longer and more exploratory?



21. Do you want checkpoints?



22. Do you want secret areas or hidden paths?



23. What obstacles do you want?



24. Should the game get hard slowly or quickly?



## Coins And Collectibles

25. What should the player collect?



26. Should the collectibles stay as coins or become something else?



27. What should collectibles do in the game?



28. How many collectibles should a normal level have?



29. Do you want rare collectibles too?



30. Should collecting everything in a level unlock a bonus?



## Mobs And Enemies

31. What kinds of enemies do you want?



32. Should enemies be animals, robots, monsters, guards, or something else?



33. Should enemies be simple or smart?



34. Can the player defeat enemies, or only avoid them?



35. If the player can defeat them, how?



36. Do you want mini-bosses or bosses?



37. Should different worlds have different enemies?



## Goals And Winning

38. How does the player finish a level?



39. What should the level goal object be: flag, door, portal, chest, rescue target, or something else?



40. What is the overall goal of the full game?



41. Is there a story reason for beating each level?



42. Do you want a final boss or ending scene?



## Sounds And Music

43. What kind of music should the game have?



44. Should each world have different music?



45. What should the jump sound like?



46. What should the collectible sound like?



47. What should enemy sounds be like?



48. What should the level win sound be like?



49. Do you want menu sound effects too?



50. Do you want voice lines, or no voices?



## Art Style

51. Do you want pixel art, cartoon art, hand-drawn art, or another style?



52. Should the game look retro or modern?



53. What colors should define the game?



54. Are there any games whose art style you want to learn from?



55. Are placeholder art and sounds okay at first?



## Game Feel

56. Should movement feel floaty or tight?



57. Should jumping be easy for beginners or more skill-based?



58. How many hits should the player take before losing?



59. Should falling off the map instantly reset the player?



60. Should the player have health, lives, or both?



## Progress And Rewards

61. Should levels unlock one by one or from a level-select screen?



62. Do you want a score screen after each level?



63. Do you want star ratings, grades, or ranks?



64. Should players unlock skins, powers, or characters?



65. Do you want progress to save?



## Story

66. Do you want a story in the game?



67. What problem is the hero trying to solve?



68. Who or what is causing that problem?



69. Should the story feel serious, funny, simple, or epic?



70. Do you want dialogue boxes or mostly gameplay?



## Build Plan

71. What should we change first: character, level art, enemies, collectibles, sounds, or goals?



72. Do you want to keep the current movement for now or change it early?



73. Should the first revamp be a small reskin or a major redesign?



74. Are you okay using temporary placeholders while we build?



75. What is the one change that would make this feel like your game?
